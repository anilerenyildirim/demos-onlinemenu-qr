For franchising

98 branches, one menu

The brand voice stays identical in every branch; branches change only what belongs to them.

  • Central menu management

    Product names, images and categories are edited once, centrally, and go live in every branch at the same moment.

  • Per-branch price, stock and hours

    Each branch sets its own price difference, sold-out items and opening hours. The central menu stays untouched.

  • Three languages

    Turkish, English and fully right-to-left Arabic. Guests pick their language with a single tap.

  • QR / NFC table cards

    Cards are printed once. When a price or product changes, nothing is reprinted; the update is live instantly.
